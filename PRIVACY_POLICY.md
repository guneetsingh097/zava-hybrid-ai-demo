# Privacy Policy – ZavaInsurance-WindowsDemo

**Last updated:** May 5, 2026

## Overview

ZavaInsurance-WindowsDemo is a demonstration application showcasing on-device AI capabilities on Windows Copilot+ PCs. This app is designed with privacy as a core principle — all AI processing happens locally on your device.

## Data Collection

**This app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.** Specifically:

- **No data is sent to external servers.** All AI inference runs entirely on your device's Neural Processing Unit (NPU) using local models.
- **No analytics or telemetry** is collected.
- **No user accounts** are required.
- **No cookies or tracking** mechanisms are used.

## Local Data Processing

- Documents you upload for analysis are processed locally on your device and stored temporarily in your local AppData folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ZavaInsurance\`).
- AI analysis is performed on-device using the Windows Copilot Runtime (Phi Silica / Foundry Local).
- Generated reports are saved locally on your device only.
- No document content or analysis results leave your device.

## Network Access

The app requires local network access (`127.0.0.1`) solely to communicate between its internal web server and user interface. No external network connections are made during normal operation.

## Data Retention

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by removing the `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ZavaInsurance\` folder.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or cloud AI providers.

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in app updates distributed through the Microsoft Store.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the Microsoft Store app listing.
