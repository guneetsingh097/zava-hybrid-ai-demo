Processed Locally. Connected Securely.

All AI inference runs on-device via the NPU — no sensitive data leaves the hardware. When connected, Microsoft WorkIQ securely enriches responses with organizational context from M365 (emails, documents, Teams). Works offline too — disconnect Wi-Fi and the AI keeps running.

💡 Why Hybrid AI for Zava?
$0.00 per inference

AI runs on the NPU — no per-token API fees. Routine tasks at zero incremental cost across your fleet.

Data Sovereignty

PII, claim photos, and policy data stay on-device. WorkIQ connects securely — only context flows in, never raw data out.

Best of Both Worlds

Local NPU for fast private inference + Microsoft WorkIQ for organizational intelligence. Hybrid AI without compromise.

Fleet-Scale Savings

10,000 adjusters × 50 daily inferences × 260 days = 130M inferences/year at $0 cloud inference cost.
