### Root Cause Analysis

**Primary cause:** Sump pump motor failure during sustained heavy rainfall.

**Contributing factors:**
1. Storm intensity exceeded sump pump capacity before failure (3.8" in 4 hours)
2. NW window well drainage overwhelmed — overflow contributed to ingress
3. Pump appears to be original (2006 install, 18 years old) — end of useful life

**Assessment:** This is a covered peril under the HO-46 water backup endorsement. The sump pump failure is the primary mechanism; window well overflow is secondary/contributing.
