# Sample Claim Photos

These photos are used for the **Claims AI Assessment** demo. They represent water damage at the insured property (Sarah Mitchell, 4218 Elm Creek Dr, Austin, TX).

## Usage

1. Open the app → **Claims AI** tab
2. Drag any photo from this folder into the upload area
3. Select "Water Damage" as damage type
4. Click **Assess with On-Device AI**

The NPU will analyze the image locally and provide:
- Damage severity assessment
- Estimated repair scope
- Recommended next steps
- Safety concerns
